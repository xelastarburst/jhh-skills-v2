## Books

### 1. Tae Kim, *The Nvidia Way: Jensen Huang and the Making of a Tech Giant* (W.W. Norton, 2024)

The definitive history of NVIDIA. The single richest source for understanding Jensen's cognitive patterns, not just his biography.

**Key contributions to the reasoning model:**
- NV1/NV2 failure stories and the RIVA 128 near-death bet — the origin of "bet bigger after failure, not smaller"
- "Speed of light" as an operating principle — benchmark against the theoretically possible, not against competitors
- Whiteboarding culture — "at the whiteboard, there is no place to hide"
- Top Five emails — how Jensen replaces status reports with direct ground-truth signals
- "Shipping the whole cow" — full-stack thinking, own every layer
- DLSS on-the-spot invention — example of first-principles reconstruction under pressure
- Hiring practices, "up or out" culture, co-founder relationships

**Supplementary reviews that extracted additional detail:**
- sobrief.com book summary
- thechipletter.substack.com review (December 2024)
- onepercentamonth.com review (December 2024)
- drorpoleg.com analysis (July 2025)

### 2. Stephen Witt, *The Thinking Machine* (2025, referenced via New Yorker profile)

New Yorker-length profile providing biographical and cultural detail. Useful for voice and personality modeling.

## Long-Form Podcast Interviews

### 3. Acquired FM — "NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang" (March 2024)
🔗 https://www.acquired.fm/episodes/jensen-huang

~2 hour interview. The single best source for Jensen reasoning out loud in real time.

**Key contributions:**
- "Zero billion dollar markets" — the concept that the best opportunities have no existing market
- CUDA on GeForce existential bet ($8B → $1.5B market cap) — committing totally when the reasoning chain holds
- Install base > elegance — why x86 beat RISC, why CUDA beat OpenCL
- "The company is a computer" — organizational architecture as product architecture
- "Mission is the boss" — not the CEO, not the board, the mission
- Fried chicken vs burgers analogy — focus and specialization

### 4. Lex Fridman Podcast #494 — "Jensen Huang: NVIDIA" (March 2026)
🔗 https://lexfridman.com/jensen-huang-transcript/

**Key contributions:**
- "Curiosity → reasoning system → conviction" — the explicit cognitive pipeline
- Extreme co-design philosophy
- 60+ direct reports organizational model
- Rack-scale thinking (not chip-scale)
- Amdahl's Law as a universal reasoning tool
- Accelerated computing thesis in full

### 5. BG2 Pod — "NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang on OpenAI, Future of Compute, and the American Dream" (2024)
🔗 https://singjupost.com/transcript-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-on-openai-future-of-compute-and-the-american-dream-bg2-pod/

**Key contributions:**
- NVIDIA-OpenAI symbiosis and platform dynamics
- Insatiable compute demand reasoning

### 6. Stanford GSB "View From The Top" — Jensen Huang on First-Principles Thinking (2024)
🔗 https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/insights/jensen-huang-how-use-first-principles-thinking-drive-decisions

## Keynote Addresses & Speeches

### 7. GTC 2025 Keynote — San Jose (March 2025)
📰 Summaries via: everestgrp.com, indianexpress.com, nvidia.com

**Key contributions:**
- "Three computers" framework: training computer, simulation computer, deployment computer
- Physical AI definition: "AI that understands friction, inertia, cause and effect"
- CUDA-X library ecosystem as platform moat
- "100x inference demand" prediction
- Three waves of AI: perception → generation → agentic
- "Two factories" vision (data factory + AI factory)
- RL with verifiable results as the breakthrough mechanism

### 8. GTC 2026 Keynote — San Jose (March 2026)
📰 Summaries via: globaldatacenterhub.com, qz.com, eweek.com, datacenterfrontier.com, nvidia.com/gtc

**Key contributions:**
- AI factory era — inference as permanent compute economy
- Agents as enterprise software layer
- Physical AI as next workload class after reasoning
- "Three markets collapsing into one" — inference economy + agents + physical AI
- Physical AI Data Factory Blueprint

### 9. CES 2026 Keynote — Las Vegas (January 2026)
📝 Full transcript via: rev.com/transcripts/nvidia-at-ces-2026

**Key contributions:**
- Cosmos world models: "reasons about edge scenarios, breaks them down into familiar physical interactions"
- Turning compute into synthetic data for AV and robotics training
- The simulation-to-reality pipeline

### 10. Stanford Commencement / SIEPR Speech (2024)

**Key contributions:**
- "One of my great advantages is that I have very low expectations"
- "People with very high expectations have very low resilience"
- Character > intelligence
- Perpetual worry even at $2T+ market cap — never complacent

### 11. Stanford Entrepreneurship Corner Speech (2011)

**Key contributions:**
- "Unless you have a tolerance for failure, you will never experiment, and if you don't ever experiment, you will never innovate."
- Early articulation of the failure-as-input philosophy
