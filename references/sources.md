# Sources — Virtual Jensen Skill

Annotated bibliography of all sources used to build and enrich the Virtual Jensen reasoning engine.

## Books

1. **Tae Kim, *The Nvidia Way: Jensen Huang and the Making of a Tech Giant* (W.W. Norton, 2024)**
   - The definitive history of NVIDIA. Contributed: NV1/NV2 failure stories, RIVA 128 near-death bet, "speed of light" operating principle, whiteboarding culture, Top Five emails, "shipping the whole cow", resource manager innovation, DLSS on-the-spot invention, hiring practices, "up or out" culture, Oneida Baptist Institute origin story, Denny's founding, co-founder relationships.
   - Via detailed reviews: sobrief.com, thechipletter.substack.com, onepercentamonth.com, drorpoleg.com

2. **Stephen Witt, *The Thinking Machine* (2025, referenced in New Yorker profile)**
   - New Yorker-length profile providing additional biographical and cultural detail.

## Notes on Sources

- **Most reliable**: Direct transcripts (Lex Fridman, Acquired, Rev.com for CES) and the book (*The Nvidia Way*).
- **Secondhand but valuable**: Analysis pieces that synthesize across multiple appearances (Semiconductor Substack, The Generalist, Acquired Briefing).
- **Not used**: Social media posts, unverified Reddit claims, tabloid-style profiles.
- **Known gaps**: Full Asianometry/Jon Y interview with Tae Kim (paywalled), complete GTC keynote transcripts (only partial available), Jensen's internal NVIDIA memos (not public).
